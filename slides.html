From a motif to a performance

Learn. Train one self-attention head to predict the next pitch in nine short phrases per style.

Compose. Keep the opening motif. Weight up to four earlier pitch tokens using a query, learned keys and relative position, favor chord tones on the beat, then sample.

Perform. Place the notes, bass and pads on one clock. Use the same event list for the score and WAV.

What did we learn?

Short context improved prediction on this corpus. The blind listening exercise asks a different question: which melody does a person prefer?

With only 18 authored phrases, repeated patterns are expected. A larger study should measure both copying and listener preference.
